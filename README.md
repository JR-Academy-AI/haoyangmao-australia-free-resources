# Free Resources for Life, Study & Tech in Australia

A practical collection for students, developers and everyday life in Australia. Each entry explains what you can use, who it suits, its limits and where to find the official source. Global online resources are included where useful to learners in Australia; this is not a list of Australia-exclusive offers.

Curated by 薅羊毛, a JR Academy deals and free-resource project. Open the [Australian free-resource and deals directory (in Chinese)](https://deals.jracademy.ai/free-resources/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=free_resources_en&utm_content=readme_intro) for participation steps, eligibility notes and related resources. Public guides can be read before registration.

Find workplace rights information and official help routes for visa holders and migrants.

- **Conditions and limits:** This is an information source, not individual legal advice. Workplace rights and visa work conditions need separate checks.

- **Checked:** 2026-10-26

## Free English learning

<a id="sbs-english"></a>

### SBS Learn English

Practise English with free podcasts, videos and exercises based on everyday Australian situations, choosing a series at your level.

- **Location:** Online / Australia
- **Conditions and limits:** These are self-study materials, not exam preparation or a score guarantee. Difficulty varies; try an Easy series first.
- **Official source:** [SBS Learn English](https://www.sbs.com.au/language/english/en/learn-english)
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26

<a id="amep"></a>

### Adult Migrant English Program (AMEP)

Eligible migrants and humanitarian entrants can access free English tuition and find a local provider through this government program.

- **Location:** Australia
- **Conditions and limits:** This is not a universal benefit for all international students or visa holders. Confirm eligibility, arrangements and available support with the official program and provider.
- **Official source:** [Adult Migrant English Program (AMEP)](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/settling-in-australia/amep/about-the-program)
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26

## Libraries and digital resources

<a id="slv"></a>

### State Library Victoria — Free Access

Victorian residents can explore the free digital library card and electronic books, journals and newspapers.

- **Location:** Victoria
- **Conditions and limits:** Residency, verification and database licence restrictions apply. This entry covers Free Access, not paid memberships or chargeable services.
- **Official source:** [State Library Victoria — Free Access](https://www.slv.vic.gov.au/membership/)
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26

<a id="slq"></a>

### State Library of Queensland — Membership

Explore free membership, ebooks, newspapers and online courses; Queensland residents can check remote-access eligibility.

- **Location:** Queensland
- **Conditions and limits:** Remote electronic resources have residency and licence restrictions; some are onsite only. Check equipment, materials and other service charges separately.
- **Official source:** [State Library of Queensland — Membership](https://www.slq.qld.gov.au/services/membership)
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26

<a id="slnsw"></a>

### State Library of New South Wales — Library card

Apply for a free State Library NSW card; NSW residents can check access to ebooks, databases and journals from home.

- **Location:** New South Wales
- **Conditions and limits:** Home eresource access is restricted to NSW residents and may require a valid NSW address. Other applicants must follow onsite collection and access rules. Check printing and other charges separately.
- **Official source:** [State Library of New South Wales — Library card](https://www.service.nsw.gov.au/transaction/apply-for-a-state-library-card)
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26

<a id="sydney-library"></a>

### City of Sydney Library

NSW residents can join for free; interstate and international visitors can check free monthly membership. Explore borrowing and onsite internet access.

- **Location:** Sydney, NSW
- **Conditions and limits:** Visitor borrowing limits apply. Printing, replacement cards, some computer use and fabrication services can carry fees; free membership does not make every service free.
- **Official source:** [City of Sydney Library](https://www.cityofsydney.nsw.gov.au/lists-maps-inventories/fees-charges-joining-using-library)
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26
