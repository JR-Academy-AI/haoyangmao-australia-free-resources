# Free Resources for Life, Study & Tech in Australia

A practical collection for students, developers and everyday life in Australia. Each entry explains what you can use, who it suits, its limits and where to find the official source. Global online resources are included where useful to learners in Australia; this is not a list of Australia-exclusive offers.

## Student tools and cloud learning

### GitHub Student Developer Pack

Apply for student developer benefits and choose tools for your projects.

- **Location:** Online
- **Conditions and limits:** Student verification is required. Partner offers have separate terms, quotas and expiry dates; they are not all permanently free.
- **Official source:** [GitHub Student Developer Pack](https://education.github.com/pack)
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26

### JetBrains Student Pack

Eligible students can apply for IDE licences for educational use.

- **Location:** Online
- **Conditions and limits:** Student verification applies. Educational licences are for non-commercial educational use. Do not assume paid courses or AI services are included.
- **Official source:** [JetBrains Student Pack](https://www.jetbrains.com/academy/student-pack/)
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26

### Azure for Students

Eligible university students can apply for a student cloud account to practise deploying projects.

- **Location:** Online
- **Conditions and limits:** This is a conditional, time-limited cloud allowance, not unlimited free hosting. Check eligibility and service quotas before applying.
- **Official source:** [Azure for Students](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-au/free/students)
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26

### AWS Educate

Explore cloud computing through free self-paced courses and hands-on labs.

- **Location:** Online
- **Conditions and limits:** Registration and provider eligibility conditions apply. Educate labs are separate from a regular paid AWS account and do not make all AWS services free.
- **Official source:** [AWS Educate](https://aws.amazon.com/education/awseducate/)
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26

## Free programming and technical learning

### Microsoft Learn

Study Microsoft technologies in modules before deciding whether to pursue a certification.

- **Location:** Online
- **Conditions and limits:** Self-paced modules can be read for free. Certification exams, instructor-led training and your own cloud resources may cost money.
- **Official source:** [Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/)
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26

### MDN Learn Web Development

Learn HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with reference documentation alongside the lessons.

- **Location:** Online
- **Conditions and limits:** This entry covers the public documentation and tutorials, not other subscription products.
- **Official source:** [MDN Learn Web Development](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Learn_web_development)
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26

### freeCodeCamp

Learn programming through exercises and projects, completing your own work against curriculum requirements.

- **Location:** Online
- **Conditions and limits:** You must complete the work yourself. Progress or certificates are not a degree, professional licence or employment guarantee.
- **Official source:** [freeCodeCamp](https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn)
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26

### Harvard CS50x

Access computer science course materials and practise with problem sets and a final project.

- **Location:** Online
- **Conditions and limits:** The open course is free to study. Paid verification, credit and other course options are separate; this is not enrolment at Harvard.
- **Official source:** [Harvard CS50x](https://cs50.harvard.edu/x/)
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26

### MIT OpenCourseWare

Read MIT open course materials and choose lectures, assignments or videos available for each course.

- **Location:** Online
- **Conditions and limits:** Available materials vary by course. Open materials do not confer MIT enrolment, credit or a degree.
- **Official source:** [MIT OpenCourseWare](https://ocw.mit.edu/)
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26

### The Python Tutorial

Practise Python syntax, data structures, modules and exceptions with the official tutorial.

- **Location:** Online
- **Conditions and limits:** Best suited to readers with basic programming knowledge. Match the documentation version to your installed Python version.
- **Official source:** [The Python Tutorial](https://docs.python.org/3/tutorial/)
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26

### Pro Git

Read the Git book online to understand commits, branches and collaboration.

- **Location:** Online
- **Conditions and limits:** The online book is free; printed editions and third-party services may cost money.
- **Official source:** [Pro Git](https://git-scm.com/book/en/v2)
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26

## Australian life and rights information

### Study Australia — Living and education costs

Use official living and education cost information as a starting point for an Australian study budget.

- **Location:** Australia
- **Conditions and limits:** The information is free, not living expenses or tuition. It does not guarantee a personal budget or visa requirements.
- **Official source:** [Study Australia — Living and education costs](https://www.studyaustralia.gov.au/en/life-in-australia/living-and-education-costs)
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26

### Fair Work Ombudsman — Visa holders and migrants

Find workplace rights information and official help routes for visa holders and migrants.

- **Location:** Australia
- **Conditions and limits:** This is an information source, not individual legal advice. Workplace rights and visa work conditions need separate checks.
- **Official source:** [Fair Work Ombudsman — Visa holders and migrants](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/find-help-for/visa-holders-migrants)
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26

### Scamwatch

Learn about common scams, warning signs and official reporting routes.

- **Location:** Australia
- **Conditions and limits:** Information and reporting do not guarantee recovery of money. Do not put personal details or transaction evidence in public issues.
- **Official source:** [Scamwatch](https://www.scamwatch.gov.au/)
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26

### ACCC — Consumers

Find official explanations of consumer rights, pricing claims and problems with goods or services.

- **Location:** Australia
- **Conditions and limits:** The public guidance is free. It does not guarantee refunds; check the appropriate agency and process for an individual dispute.
- **Official source:** [ACCC — Consumers](https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers)
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26

## Libraries and digital resources

### State Library Victoria — Free Access

Victorian residents can explore the free digital library card and electronic books, journals and newspapers.

- **Location:** Victoria
- **Conditions and limits:** Residency, verification and database licence restrictions apply. This entry covers Free Access, not paid memberships or chargeable services.
- **Official source:** [State Library Victoria — Free Access](https://www.slv.vic.gov.au/membership/)
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26

### State Library of Queensland — Membership

Explore free membership, ebooks, newspapers and online courses; Queensland residents can check remote-access eligibility.

- **Location:** Queensland
- **Conditions and limits:** Remote electronic resources have residency and licence restrictions; some are onsite only. Check equipment, materials and other service charges separately.
- **Official source:** [State Library of Queensland — Membership](https://www.slq.qld.gov.au/services/membership)
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26

## Free galleries and museums

### Australian Museum

Visit the general galleries in Sydney for free and choose natural history and cultural collections to explore.

- **Location:** Sydney, NSW
- **Conditions and limits:** General admission is free; major exhibitions may charge. Transport, parking and food are not included.
- **Official source:** [Australian Museum](https://australian.museum/visit/)
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26

### Queensland Museum Kurilpa

Plan a free general-admission visit to the museum galleries in Brisbane.

- **Location:** Brisbane, Queensland
- **Conditions and limits:** SparkLab, some exhibitions and events charge admission. Check opening arrangements; not every experience is free.
- **Official source:** [Queensland Museum Kurilpa](https://www.museum.qld.gov.au/kurilpa/plan-your-visit/hours-and-admission)
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26

### National Gallery of Victoria

Visit general collection displays at NGV International or NGV Australia in Melbourne for free.

- **Location:** Melbourne, Victoria
- **Conditions and limits:** Special exhibitions may charge and some galleries may close temporarily. Check current exhibitions and access before visiting.
- **Official source:** [National Gallery of Victoria](https://www.ngv.vic.gov.au/plan-your-visit/)
- **Checked:** 2026-10-26
