# Free half-day museum and gallery plans in Australia

HaoYangMao / 薅羊毛 · Original editorial guide · 26 October 2026 · CC BY 4.0

Confirm current conditions with the provider; this is not proof of approval.

## Sydney — Australian Museum

Choose the Australian Museum permanent exhibitions. Check opening and admissions, select one subject from the visitor map, allow a personal break, then decide whether to see another general gallery. General entry is free; major exhibitions and programs may charge. Transport and food are separate. These are editorial half-day suggestions, not measured routes or actual visit records. Check current accessibility, closures and booking rules before travelling.

[Official visit information](https://australian.museum/visit/admission/)

## Melbourne — NGV International

Choose NGV International on St Kilda Road. Check actual gallery access first, select two open collection areas and take a break between them. Some spaces are temporarily closed for installation according to the reviewed visitor page. General entry is free; special exhibitions, transport and food may cost money. These are editorial half-day suggestions, not measured routes or actual visit records. Check current accessibility, closures and booking rules before travelling.

[Official visit information](https://www.ngv.vic.gov.au/plan-your-visit/) · [Additional official venue](https://www.acmi.net.au/whats-on/story-of-the-moving-image-exhibition/)

## Brisbane — Queensland Museum Kurilpa

Start with general galleries at Queensland Museum Kurilpa. Check separately for paid SparkLab and special exhibitions. After a break, follow official maps and onsite signs to select a free display at QAG or GOMA in the South Bank cultural precinct. Shorten the plan to one venue if needed. Transport, parking, food and special programs are separate costs. These are editorial half-day suggestions, not measured routes or actual visit records. Check current accessibility, closures and booking rules before travelling.

[Official visit information](https://www.museum.qld.gov.au/kurilpa/plan-your-visit/hours-and-admission) · [Additional official venue](https://www.qagoma.qld.gov.au/visit/)
