## GitHub Student Developer Pack

Use GitHub for project history and collaboration. Check student verification first, apply through your own account, then inspect each partner offer before activating it. Acceptance into Education does not activate every benefit or make every service permanently free.

Source: https://education.github.com/pack

## JetBrains educational licence

Choose an IDE only if your coursework needs it. Check eligibility, apply through the official student route and record renewal requirements. Educational licences cannot be used for commercial work; verify the appropriate licence before client or workplace delivery.

Source: https://sales.jetbrains.com/hc/en-gb/articles/207241195-Do-you-offer-free-educational-licenses-for-students-and-teachers

## Azure for Students

Check eligibility and the current student allowance, apply through the official student account route, and inspect costs before creating resources. Credits are not cash or unlimited hosting. Review and remove unused resources after the exercise; do not switch to a paid account just to bypass limits.

Source: https://azure.microsoft.com/en-au/free/students

## Figma for Education

Check structured-course and education verification requirements, verify your own account, then confirm team and file permissions before collaborating. Do not assume a commercial team qualifies because one member is a student. Check paid add-ons separately.

Source: https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/360041061214-Figma-for-Education

## Notion education plan

Check recognised higher-education eligibility, your primary school email and the one-member workspace requirement. Review the desktop or web upgrade route. Guest collaboration and student organisations have separate rules; read plan-change consequences before changing membership or subscriptions.

Source: https://www.notion.com/help/notion-for-education
